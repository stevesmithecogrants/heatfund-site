More about us
UK energy grants available in 2026 include the Boiler Upgrade Scheme (£7,500 towards a heat pump in England and Wales, or £9,000 for an air or ground source heat pump in oil- or LPG-heated homes with no mains gas until 31 March 2027), the Warm Homes: Local Grant, 0% VAT until 31 March 2027, the Warm Home Discount (£150 off electricity bills) and ECO4, which closes on 31 December 2026. Scotland has Home Energy Scotland and Warmer Homes Scotland, and Wales has Nest.

Straight answers

Do I have to qualify for a grant to use this?

No. The guides and the eligibility check are open to every homeowner. If a grant doesn't cover your upgrade, we'll say so and show what it really costs, so you can still decide whether it's worth doing.

Am I eligible for free home improvements?

Possibly — through ECO4 or the Warm Homes: Local Grant, which can fully fund insulation and heating for eligible households. ECO4 is for low-income and benefit households and work must be finished by 31 December 2026. The Warm Homes: Local Grant covers households on £36,000 or less, a qualifying benefit, or in a low-income postcode, through participating councils. Run the check and we'll confirm which route fits.

How much can I save with solar panels?

A 4kW solar system is worth about £630 to £760 a year at October 2026 prices without a battery (DESNZ, PVGIS), and a typical installed system costs £6,600 to £7,800 with 0% VAT until 31 March 2027. What you save depends on your roof, how much electricity you use during the day, and whether you add a battery.

What is the ECO4 scheme?

ECO4 is an energy supplier scheme that funds insulation and heating upgrades for low-income and benefit households. It's based on household eligibility and property efficiency, and it closes on 31 December 2026 — work has to be finished by then.

How does the Boiler Upgrade Scheme work?

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme gives £7,500 off a heat pump in England and Wales — £9,000 for an air or ground source heat pump in oil- or LPG-heated homes with no mains gas until 31 March 2027, and £2,500 off air-to-air once MCS certifies installers. There's no income test and no EPC needed since 28 April 2026. An MCS-certified installer applies for the grant on your behalf, and there's one grant per property.

Do I need planning permission for solar panels?

For most homes, rooftop solar panels are permitted development and don't need planning permission. Extra rules can apply to listed buildings and some conservation areas, and a certified installer will confirm the position for your home before any work starts.

How long does a heat pump installation take?

Typically 2–3 days for an air source system once the survey and design are done. Whether radiators or pipework need changing is settled at the survey, before you commit to anything.
