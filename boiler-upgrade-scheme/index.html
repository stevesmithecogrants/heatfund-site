Boiler Upgrade Scheme: £7,500 off (up to £9,000 off-gas)

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme helps homeowners in England and Wales replace a fossil-fuel boiler with a cleaner heating system. The grant is paid towards the installation cost and is claimed for you by an MCS-certified installer — you do not apply for the money yourself.

Funding summary

In short: £7,500 for a heat pump, £9,000 away from gas

How much is the grant?

The amount depends on the system you install. These are the published Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant levels:

£7,500
Air source & ground source heat pumps

Towards an air source or ground source heat pump replacing a fossil-fuel system.

£2,500
Air-to-air heat pumps

Towards an eligible air-to-air heat pump.

£5,000
Biomass boilers

Towards a biomass boiler, where the property and system meet the scheme rules.

£9,000
Off-gas-grid uplift

For eligible off-gas-grid homes currently heated by oil or LPG, until 31 March 2027.

The most important facts

Your installer claims itAn MCS-certified installer applies for the grant on your behalf and deducts the amount from your quote. You should not be asked to claim it yourself.
One claim per propertyThe Boiler Upgrade Scheme allows one claim per property. If a property has already had a grant under the scheme, a second claim for the same property is not available.
No EPC requirementSince 28 April 2026 there is no EPC requirement for the scheme — you no longer need a particular EPC rating, or a recent EPC with insulation recommendations, to qualify on that point.
You must own the propertyThe scheme is for property owners in England and Wales, including homeowners and landlords, replacing an existing fossil-fuel heating system.
The system must replace fossil fuelThe new system must replace a gas, oil, LPG or direct electric system. Replacing an existing heat pump with another heat pump is not covered.
£9,000 uplift is time-limitedThe higher £9,000 level applies to eligible off-gas-grid oil and LPG homes until 31 March 2027. After that date the published uplift may change, so check before you rely on it.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant?

£7,500 towards an air source or ground source heat pump, £2,500 towards an air-to-air heat pump, and £5,000 towards a biomass boiler. Eligible off-gas-grid homes moving away from oil or LPG can get £9,000 until 31 March 2027.

Do I need an EPC to qualify?

No. The EPC requirement was removed from 28 April 2026, so a particular EPC rating is no longer a condition of the grant.

Who applies for the grant — me or the installer?

Your installer does. An MCS-certified installer applies on your behalf and takes the grant off the price in your quote.

Can I claim twice for the same home?

No. The scheme allows one claim per property.

Can I replace my existing heat pump with the grant?

No. The grant is for replacing a fossil-fuel or direct electric system, not for swapping one heat pump for another.

What does a heat pump cost after the grant?

As an example, an 8kW air source system at £11,250 would leave £3,500 to pay after a £7,500 grant, and the median air source installation of £12,826 would leave approx. £5,326. Your actual price depends on a survey of your home.

Is VAT charged on the installation?

Domestic energy-saving installations currently benefit from 0% VAT until March 2027. See our VAT Relief guide for how that interacts with your quote.

Does the grant guarantee I will qualify?

No. HeatFund only checks whether you may qualify. Final eligibility is confirmed through the installer’s application under the scheme rules.
