How HeatFund checks and updates its content

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Written by the HeatFund editorial team · Checked against GOV.UK and Ofgem guidance

Sources first

Grant amounts, deadlines and eligibility rules are taken from official sources — GOV.UK, Ofgem, HMRC guidance and the Scottish and Welsh scheme operators — and each guide carries a Sources & data section and a last-updated date.

Our own words

We write every guide ourselves. Where we describe a scheme we use the official facts, checked against the source, and set out plainly where a figure is an estimate or a typical range.

Corrections

If you spot something you believe is wrong or out of date, email info@heatfund.co.uk with the page and the issue. Verified errors are corrected and the updated date on the page is changed.

No paid placements

Installers cannot pay to be recommended in our editorial content. The site is free for readers; how HeatFund is funded is explained on the homepage FAQ.
