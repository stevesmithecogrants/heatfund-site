Sources & Data

This checker is built from the published rules of the UK's current energy schemes. Figures and criteria are taken from official sources and may change — always check the source for the latest position.

  • GOV.UK — Boiler Upgrade Scheme: £7,500 towards an air or ground source heat pump, £5,000 towards a biomass boiler.
  • Ofgem — Energy Company Obligation (ECO4): funded insulation and heating measures for eligible households, running to 31 December 2026.
  • GOV.UK — Find an energy certificate: the official EPC register used for the Step 4 lookup.
  • GOV.UK — Warm Homes: Local Grant: income and postcode-based funding for homes with an EPC rating of D–G.
